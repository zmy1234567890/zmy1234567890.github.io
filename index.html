Research

Single-image reflection removal for tinted vehicle glass

2026 – present · primary project

Ordinary glass returns a bright, well-behaved reflection; a tinted car window does not. It reflects very little and, where the photographer blocks the light path, the reflected content is replaced by something dark while the illumination also drops. I built a controlled window-glass rig and collected paired captures (reflection-contaminated input, occluder-based reflection-free reference) across indoor and outdoor scenes, with a frozen 15-scene test panel.

  • A region-based training term is worth +3–4 dB over no region term, but where the region is drawn makes no significant difference — so the gain does not come from occluder-specific structure.
  • The global photometric level of the transmission layer is not identifiable from a single frame; the honest comparison therefore needs a reference and a per-image calibration.
  • Automatic occluder-region masks are not measurement-grade: threshold criteria either shrink to the darkest core or drift over the scene, and cross-domain localisation is close to chance.

Spatio-temporal gradient basis decomposition for infrared small targets

2024 – 2026 · second author

STGBD-Net replaces attention-style feature fusion with gradient basis decomposition: eight directional gradient kernels span the basis, and a light three-stage U-shaped backbone keeps the model at 0.27 M parameters / 1.51 GFLOPs in the single-frame variant. The multi-frame variant adds a temporal gradient module that uses inter-frame differences as temporal bases, suppressing static background while enhancing moving targets.
